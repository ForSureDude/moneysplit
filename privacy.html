Privacy Policy

MoneySplit is a financial planning calculator. The core calculator performs calculations in your browser and does not require an account.

Information you enter

Amounts and categories entered into the calculator are processed locally by the browser in the current version. The share feature encodes the calculation into a URL so that the same calculation can be reopened. Do not place sensitive personal information into category names.
